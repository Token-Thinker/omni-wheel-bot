Controls

  • Tap and drag anywhere — joystick spawns at your finger, deflection in any direction sends a drive command.
  • Release — joystick disappears, robot stops.
  • Speed grows from the spawn point outward. The outer ring marks 100%; the inner mark is just visual.
  • Long-press a toggle in this drawer to switch its state on or off.
  • Green dot in the top-left = WebSocket connected. Red = closed/error. Grey = connecting.
  • You must reach the connected state before the controller will move the robot.
